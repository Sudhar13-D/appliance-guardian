# Appliance Guardian

Build a modern, clean, professional startup website for a company called [Your Startup Name].

One-line description: AI-powered predictive maintenance and health diagnostics for home appliances — starting with embedded manufacturer partnerships to bring Samsung/LG-grade smart diagnostics to India's mass-market appliance brands.

Tone: Confident, technical but simple, trustworthy, safety-focused. Target audience includes potential manufacturer partners, investors/judges, and early adopter consumers.

Color scheme: Deep blue (#1A3C6E or similar) as primary, with a warm orange/amber accent (#F2994A) for CTAs and highlights. White background, plenty of whitespace, sans-serif fonts (Inter, Poppins, or similar).

Pages/Sections needed (single-page scroll site):

1. Hero Section

Headline: "Give Every Appliance a Health Report Card"

Subheadline: "AI-powered fault detection and predictive maintenance — built for India's next generation of smart appliances."

Primary CTA button: "Partner With Us" (links to contact form)

Secondary CTA: "See How It Works" (scrolls to solution section)

Include a clean hero image/illustration of a fan, iron, or heater with a subtle glowing "health scan" visual effect.

2. The Problem Section

Headline: "90% of Indian Appliances Are Still 'Blind'"

3 short stat cards:

"Only ~8-10% smart appliance penetration in India"

"No early warning before breakdown, shock, or fire risk"

"Millions in avoidable repair and replacement costs every year"

3. Our Solution Section

Headline: "Module-Level Diagnostics, Built Into the Appliance"

Short paragraph: We work with appliance manufacturers to embed sensor logging and AI diagnostics directly into new product lines — tracking voltage, temperature, and current at each internal module (power supply, heating coil, motor) to detect and localize faults before they cause failure.

3-column icon layout:

Sense (icon: sensor chip) — "Real-time voltage, temperature, and current logging at each module"

Diagnose (icon: AI brain) — "AI models compare live data against safe operating baselines"

Alert (icon: phone notification) — "Instant, plain-language health reports sent to the user's phone"

4. Why Now / Market Opportunity Section

Headline: "A ₹350B+ Market With Massive Room to Grow"

Include a simple stat callout: "India's smart appliance market is projected to grow from ₹223B to ₹353B by FY2029 — yet most mass-market brands still have zero diagnostic intelligence."

Subtext: "We're bringing embedded AI diagnostics to the appliance brands and price segments that global players ignore."

5. For Manufacturers Section (B2B focus)

Headline: "Partner With Us to Build Smarter Appliances"

Bullet points:

Faster fault localization reduces warranty and service costs

New "self-diagnosing" feature for product differentiation

Proven bench-level diagnostic logic ready for pilot integration

CTA: "Schedule a Pilot Discussion"

6. Roadmap / Vision Section

Simple horizontal timeline with 3 stages:

Phase 1: Bench-level proof of concept (fault logging & localization)

Phase 2: Manufacturer pilot integration

Phase 3: Scale across appliance categories and bigger industrial machines

7. Team Section

Placeholder cards for founder(s) — name, role, short bio, LinkedIn icon.

8. Contact / Get In Touch Section

Simple form: Name, Email, Organization, Message

Also display: email address, phone (optional), location "Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India"

9. Footer

Logo, tagline, quick links (Home, Problem, Solution, Team, Contact), social icons, copyright line.

Additional technical requirements:

Fully responsive (mobile-first)

Smooth scroll navigation with a sticky top navbar (Logo | Problem | Solution | For Manufacturers | Team | Contact | CTA button)

Use subtle scroll animations (fade-in on scroll) for each section

Keep total page load lightweight — no heavy background videos
